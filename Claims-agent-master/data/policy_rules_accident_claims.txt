claim adjudication. All decisions made by automated claim processing systems,
human adjusters, and third-party agents must conform to the rules herein.
Any deviation requires written approval from the Claims Review Authority (CRA).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 — DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For the purpose of this document, the following terms are defined as follows:

  Policyholder       : The individual named in the insurance contract.
  Insured Party      : Policyholder and any dependents covered under the plan.
  Accident           : A sudden, unforeseeable event causing bodily injury or
                       property damage, not resulting from intentional acts.
  Claimant           : The insured party filing the claim.
  Deductible         : The fixed amount the claimant pays before coverage applies.
  Coverage Limit     : The maximum amount payable by Nova Shield for a claim.
  Waiting Period     : Minimum policy tenure before a claim type becomes eligible.
  Co-payment (Co-pay): Percentage of approved claim amount borne by the claimant.
  Adjuster           : Licensed Nova Shield representative who evaluates claims.
  FIR                : First Information Report filed with law enforcement.
  IDV                : Insured Declared Value — the market value of the vehicle.
  Pre-authorization  : Approval obtained from Nova Shield before incurring expense.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 — GENERAL CLAIM ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A claim will be considered valid and processed only when ALL of the following
general conditions are satisfied:

  GC-01  The policy must be active and premium payments current at the time of
         the accident. Lapsed policies are ineligible for any claim.

  GC-02  The accident must be reported to Nova Shield within 48 hours of
         occurrence via the designated claims hotline (1800-NOVA-CLM) or the
         Nova Shield mobile application.

  GC-03  For accidents involving motor vehicles, a copy of the FIR or Motor
         Accident Report (MAR) filed with local police must be submitted within
         7 calendar days.

  GC-04  The claimant must not be under the influence of alcohol or controlled
         substances at the time of the accident. Toxicology reports may be
         requested by the adjuster.

  GC-05  The policy must have been active for a minimum of 30 days (waiting
         period) before any claim is eligible, unless the accident occurs during
         initial travel covered by the policy start date.

  GC-06  Fraudulent claims, misrepresentation of facts, or suppression of
         material information will result in immediate claim rejection and
         possible legal action.

  GC-07  The claimant must cooperate fully with the assigned adjuster, including
         providing access to medical records, repair estimates, and any other
         documentation deemed necessary.

  GC-08  Claims must be fully submitted (all documentation) within 30 calendar
         days of the accident. Late submissions require written justification
         and are subject to CRA approval.

SECTION 4 — STANDARD DEDUCTIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL CLAIMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following deductions apply uniformly across all claim types unless
explicitly stated otherwise in the scenario-specific rules in Section 3.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1  COMPULSORY DEDUCTIBLE (CD)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The compulsory deductible is a fixed first-loss borne by the claimant,
subtracted before any claim benefit is computed.

  Plan Tier     | Medical Claims CD | Vehicle Claims CD | Property Claims CD
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Silver         | INR 2,000         | INR 1,000          | INR 1,500
  Gold           | INR 1,500         | INR 1,000          | INR 1,000
  Platinum       | INR 1,000         | INR 500            | INR 500
  Commercial     | INR 2,500         | INR 2,000          | INR 2,000
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Note: Compulsory deductible is non-negotiable and cannot be waived.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.2  VOLUNTARY DEDUCTIBLE (VD)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Policyholders who opted for a voluntary deductible at policy inception
receive a premium discount. The VD amount is deducted in addition to the
compulsory deductible.

  VD Chosen at Inception | Premium Discount | VD Applied Per Claim
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  INR 2,000               | 5%               | INR 2,000
  INR 5,000               | 10%              | INR 5,000
  INR 10,000              | 15%              | INR 10,000
  INR 15,000              | 20%              | INR 15,000
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.3  CO-PAYMENT (CO-PAY)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Co-payment is a percentage of the approved claim amount borne by the
claimant after deductibles are applied. Co-pay percentages by plan tier:

  Plan Tier     | Network Hospital | Non-Network Hospital | OPD
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Silver         | 10%              | 20%                  | 20%
  Gold           | 5%               | 15%                  | 10%
  Platinum       | 0%               | 10%                  | 5%
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Co-pay is not applicable on Personal Accident lump-sum benefits
(Accidental Death, PTD, PPD).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.4  DEPRECIATION DEDUCTIONS (VEHICLE CLAIMS)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

For motor vehicle claims, depreciation is applied to replaced parts
based on the vehicle's age. Labour charges are not subject to depreciation.

  Vehicle Age           | Depreciation Rate
  ──────────────────────────────────────────
  Less than 6 months    | 0%
  6 months to 1 year    | 5%
  1 year to 2 years     | 10%
  2 years to 3 years    | 15%
  3 years to 4 years    | 25%
  4 years to 5 years    | 35%
  5 years to 10 years   | 40%
  Over 10 years         | 50%
  ──────────────────────────────────────────

Additional depreciation rates for specific components (regardless of age):
  - Tyres, tubes, batteries : 50%
  - Plastic / fibre parts   : 30%
  - Nylon / rubber parts    : 50%
  - Glass / windshield      : 0% (no depreciation)

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.5  NON-MEDICAL EXPENSES (NME) DEDUCTIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The following charges are excluded from hospitalization claim calculations
as they are considered non-medical in nature:

  - Registration / administrative fees     : Excluded in full
  - Telephone / internet charges           : Excluded in full
  - Guest meals / attendant meals          : Excluded in full
  - Laundry charges                        : Excluded in full
  - Personal comfort items (TV, newspaper) : Excluded in full
  - Cosmetic / grooming products           : Excluded in full
  - Over-the-counter vitamins (non-Rx)     : Excluded in full

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.6  WAITING PERIOD DEDUCTIONS / INELIGIBILITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  - Claims filed within 30 days of policy inception: Not payable (GC-05).
  - Pre-existing condition-linked events: Not payable (see Section 6).
  - Specific illness waiting periods do not apply to pure accident claims.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.7  LATE REPORTING PENALTY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Claims reported after the mandatory 48-hour window but before 7 days:
  - 10% reduction of approved claim amount.

Claims reported after 7 days but before 30 days (with valid justification):
  - 25% reduction of approved claim amount.
  - Approval by CRA required.

Claims reported after 30 days: Not payable without exceptional CRA waiver.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.8  FRAUD AND MISREPRESENTATION DEDUCTIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

If material misrepresentation is found post-settlement:
  - Full claim amount to be recovered with 12% annual interest.
  - Policy may be cancelled without refund of premium.
  - Claimant may be referred to the Insurance Fraud Investigation Bureau.

SECTION 5 — CLAIM COMPUTATION EXAMPLE

  Repair Estimate (approved by surveyor)   : INR 45,000
  Less: Depreciation on parts (25%)        : INR 8,000  (on parts worth INR 32,000)
  Labour charges (no depreciation)         : INR 13,000 (included in estimate above)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Post-depreciation amount                 : INR 37,000
  Less: Compulsory Deductible (Gold)       : INR 1,000
  Less: Voluntary Deductible (if INR 5,000): INR 5,000
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Net Payable by Nova Shield               : INR 31,000

EXAMPLE: Hospitalization Claim (Silver Plan, Network Hospital)

  Approved hospital bill                   : INR 80,000
  Less: Non-medical expenses               : INR 2,000
  Less: Compulsory Deductible              : INR 2,000
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Sub-total                                : INR 76,000
  Less: Co-pay 10% (Silver, Network)       : INR 7,600
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Net Payable by Nova Shield               : INR 68,400

SECTION 6 — EXCLUSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following events and conditions are EXCLUDED from coverage. Claims arising
in whole or in part from these causes will be rejected:

  EX-01  Intentional self-inflicted injury or suicide attempt.
  EX-02  Injuries sustained while committing a criminal offense.
  EX-03  Accidents while under influence of alcohol (BAC > 0.03%) or drugs.
  EX-04  War, terrorism, nuclear/biological/chemical events.
  EX-05  Participation in motor racing, stunts, or extreme sports unless
         Adventure Sports Add-on was purchased.
  EX-06  Pre-existing physical conditions that directly caused the accident
         (e.g., epileptic seizure while driving causing a collision).
  EX-07  Mental health disorders as the primary cause of injury.
  EX-08  Accidents during aviation (other than as a ticketed commercial passenger).
  EX-09  Injuries sustained outside the policy's territorial limits (India,
         unless International Travel Add-on is active).
  EX-10  Damage or injury caused by gradual wear, mechanical breakdown, or
         structural defect not triggered by a sudden accidental event.
  EX-11  Cosmetic treatment or elective surgery arising post-accident (only
         medically necessary reconstructive procedures are covered).
  EX-12  Experimental treatments or procedures not approved by MCI / ICMR.
  EX-13  HIV/AIDS-related treatment unless injury was caused by accidental
         contamination (e.g., needle-stick injury to a healthcare worker).

SECTION 7 — CLAIM FILING PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 — NOTIFICATION
  Contact: 1800-NOVA-CLM (toll-free, 24x7) or nova-claims@novashield.in
  Timeline: Within 48 hours of accident.
  Provide: Policy number, claimant name, date/time/location of accident,
           brief description, nature of injury or damage.

STEP 2 — CLAIM REGISTRATION
  A Claim Reference Number (CRN) will be issued within 2 hours of notification.
  Retain the CRN for all subsequent communication.

STEP 3 — DOCUMENT SUBMISSION
  Submit within 30 days of accident via:
  - Nova Shield mobile app (photo upload)
  - Email: claims-docs@novashield.in (with CRN in subject)
  - Physical submission at nearest Nova Shield branch

  Standard documents (all claims):
  - Duly filled Claim Form NS-CF-01
  - Government-issued photo ID of claimant
  - Copy of insurance policy schedule
  - FIR / MAR (if accident involves vehicles or public space)

  Additional documents by claim type:
  - Medical claims   : Hospital bills, discharge summary, prescriptions,
                       diagnostic reports, treating doctor's certificate.
  - Vehicle claims   : RC book, driving license, repair estimate from
                       authorised workshop, surveyor report.
  - Death claims     : Death certificate, post-mortem report, nominee ID.
  - Disability claims: Disability certificate from two specialists,
                       employer's loss-of-income certificate.

STEP 4 — ASSESSMENT
  - Adjuster assigned within 24 hours of document submission.
  - Surveyor visit (vehicle/property): Within 48 hours of assignment.
  - Query response turnaround: 5 working days from query date.

STEP 5 — DECISION
  - Decision timeline: 15 working days from complete document submission.
  - Cashless claims: Authorization within 4 hours for network hospitals.
  - Repudiated claims: Written reason provided; claimant may appeal within
    30 days by writing to: grievances@novashield.in

STEP 6 — SETTLEMENT
  - NEFT payment to registered bank account within 7 working days of approval.
  - Cheque issuance (if NEFT unavailable): Within 10 working days.

SECTION 9 — POLICY RENEWAL AND CONTINUITY BENEFITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Claims history carries forward in the Bonus/Malus system (see below).
  - Continuous renewal without a claim entitles the policyholder to a
    No-Claim Bonus (NCB) discount on the Own Damage premium component.

  No-Claim Bonus Schedule:
  ─────────────────────────────────────
  Claim-Free Years | NCB Discount
  ─────────────────────────────────────
  1 year           | 20%
  2 years          | 25%
  3 years          | 35%
  4 years          | 45%
  5+ years         | 50%
  ─────────────────────────────────────

  NCB is forfeited upon making an Own Damage claim.
  NCB is transferable to a new policy if switching insurer.

  Malus (Claim Loading):
  - Making 2 or more OD claims in a policy year: 10% loading on renewal premium.
  - Making 3 or more OD claims in a policy year: 20% loading on renewal premium.

SECTION 10 — SPECIAL NOTES FOR AUTOMATED CLAIM PROCESSING SYSTEMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This section is intended for claim bots, AI-assisted adjudication systems,
and integrated claim management platforms using this document as a knowledge base.

  SN-01  Always verify policy status (active/lapsed) before responding to any
         claim query. Do not proceed if policy is lapsed.

  SN-02  Confirm the accident reporting timestamp against the 48-hour rule
         before assigning a coverage category. Apply late-reporting penalty
         rules from Section 4.7 automatically.

  SN-03  Cross-reference the plan tier (Silver / Gold / Platinum / Commercial)
         to apply the correct deductible and co-pay rates.

  SN-04  For vehicle age-based depreciation, compute vehicle age from the
         Registration Certificate (RC) date to the accident date.

  SN-05  The claim limit check must use the per-incident limit AND the
         annual aggregate. Reject overage automatically and inform claimant
         of residual limit.

  SN-06  Escalate to human adjuster if: (a) claim involves death or PTD,
         (b) total claim amount exceeds INR 2,00,000, (c) FIR has not been
         filed within required period, or (d) the claimant disputes a deduction.

  SN-07  Do NOT provide legal advice, fault determination, or criminal
         liability opinions. Redirect such queries to the legal helpdesk.

  SN-08  For ambiguous scenarios not covered in Sections 3 or 6, default
         response: "This scenario requires review by a licensed adjuster.
         Your claim has been escalated. CRN: [assigned number]."

  SN-09  Maintain audit logs of all claim interactions for a minimum of 7 years
         as per IRDAI data retention guidelines.

  SN-10  This document is version-controlled. Always check the document version
         header. Reject queries referencing an older policy version.
